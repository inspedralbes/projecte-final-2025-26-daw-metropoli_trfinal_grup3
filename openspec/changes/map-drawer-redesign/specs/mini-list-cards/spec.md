## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Mini Route and Collection Cards
The map drawer SHALL use simplified, small-scale versions of route and collection cards to display lists.

#### Scenario: Navigating to a route from a mini-card
- **WHEN** the user clicks the arrow icon on a "My Lists" mini-card
- **THEN** the map SHALL automatically focus on that route/collection

### Requirement: Simplified Discovery Cards
The discovery section in the map drawer SHALL display mini-cards styled after the community cards on the Home page, but with reduced information.

#### Scenario: Viewing discovery details
- **WHEN** the user interacts with a discovery mini-card
- **THEN** the system SHALL navigate the user to the corresponding route or location on the map
